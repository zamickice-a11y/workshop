FAQ

Quick answers customers usually ask.

How does booking work?

Use the booking form. We confirm the time, location, and estimate by SMS or email — usually within a few hours.

Do you provide invoices?

Yes. You'll receive a tax invoice/receipt with notes, photos, and recommendations — useful for resale and your own records.

Do you specialise in Japanese cars?

Yes. Around 60% of our work is on Japanese vehicles — Toyota, Mazda, Honda, Nissan, Subaru, Mitsubishi. We also handle European brands (VW, Audi, BMW, Mercedes) and most common passenger vehicles.

Do you do pre-purchase inspections?

Yes — that's one of our most-requested services. We come to the seller's location, do a full mechanical and electronic inspection, and give you a clear written report before you buy. More details here.

พูดภาษาไทยได้ไหม / Do you speak Thai?

Do you work on all makes & models?

Most common passenger vehicles. Some specialist work (heavy truck, exotic, full transmission rebuilds) we'll point you to a workshop. Send us your rego or VIN and we'll confirm.

What payment methods?

Visa, MasterCard, Eftpos, AMEX, JCB, Union Pay, bank transfer, and cash.

What areas do you cover?

Most of metro Adelaide — CBD, Norwood, Glenelg, Modbury, Marion, Magill, Unley, Prospect, Mile End, Burnside, Henley Beach, Salisbury, Tea Tree Gully, and surrounding suburbs. If you're not sure, just ask.
